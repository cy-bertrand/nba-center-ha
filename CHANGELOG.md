# Changelog

All notable changes to NBA Center HomeAssistant are documented here.

## [1.2.2] — 2026-11-26

### Improved
- Improved Score Timeline readability with larger axis labels, period labels and hover tooltip text.
- Enlarged the hover tooltip to keep the score and period/time information clear.

### Included from previous releases
- v1.2.1: Game leaders in Match Center (points, rebounds and assists) are clickable and open the corresponding player profile.
- v1.2.0: Added the Score Timeline chart showing home score minus away score over elapsed game time, including overtime.

## [1.2.1]
- Made game leaders clickable in Match Center.

## [1.2.0]
- Added the Match Center Score Timeline chart.
